# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

Smart Calendar respects your privacy. This policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data we store

- **Local data:** Tasks, calendar blocks, focus zones, themes, and preferences are stored on your device by default.
- **Cloud data (Pro):** If you enable cloud sync, your planner data is stored encrypted in our Convex backend, associated with your account.
- **Connected calendars:** If you connect Google Calendar or Outlook, event data syncs between Smart Calendar and the provider you authorize.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your calendar for advertising.
- We do not share your events with third parties except the calendar providers you explicitly connect.

## Encryption

Calendar data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on our servers.
